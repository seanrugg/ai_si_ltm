# Episodes

`GET /api/admin/profile/episodes?member=<name>&since=&until=` (or `swarm=`; admin only) shows what a member was doing at any moment. It derives **episodes** on lanes (`mind`, `voice`, `attention`, `body`, `motion`, `power`, `behavior`, plus `animating` and `wheels` for members that send those), one **pulse** per record with its transit time (`received_at - ts`), the `volts` and `wheel_mmps` series, and the self-report **heartbeat**, from records at read time. Nothing new is stored, so old records work and the derivation can improve later. Device knowledge lives in the tag tables at the top of `server/services/episodes.py`: a flag's `on`/`off` pair (`state-change` events) or an `episode-start`/`episode-end` pair becomes one episode; an episode still open at the end of the range runs to its end and is marked `{"open": true}`; lengths that are guesses, such as a reply's speaking time, are marked `{"estimated": true}`. A record no rule matches is a plain pulse on no lane, so another robot plugs in by its tags, not by new code. At most 20,000 records are read per call, oldest first (`truncated: true` beyond that).
